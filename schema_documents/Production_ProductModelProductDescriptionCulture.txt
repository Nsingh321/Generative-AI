Table: Production.ProductModelProductDescriptionCulture
Description: Cross-reference table mapping product descriptions and the language the description is written in.
Columns: ProductModelID (int) - (Primary key. Foreign key to ProductModel.ProductModelID.), ProductDescriptionID (int) - (Primary key. Foreign key to ProductDescription.ProductDescriptionID.), CultureID (nchar) - (Culture identification number. Foreign key to Culture.CultureID.), ModifiedDate (datetime) - (Date and time the record was last updated.)
Relationships:
ProductModelID -> Production.ProductModel.ProductModelID
ProductDescriptionID -> Production.ProductDescription.ProductDescriptionID
CultureID -> Production.Culture.CultureID
