Table: Person.PersonPhone
Description: Telephone number and type of a person.
Columns: BusinessEntityID (int) - (Business entity identification number. Foreign key to Person.BusinessEntityID.), PhoneNumber (Phone) - (Telephone number identification number.), PhoneNumberTypeID (int) - (Kind of phone number. Foreign key to PhoneNumberType.PhoneNumberTypeID.), ModifiedDate (datetime) - (Date and time the record was last updated.)
Relationships:
BusinessEntityID -> Person.Person.BusinessEntityID
PhoneNumberTypeID -> Person.PhoneNumberType.PhoneNumberTypeID
