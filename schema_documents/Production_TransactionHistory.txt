Table: Production.TransactionHistory
Description: Record of each purchase order, sales order, or work order transaction year to date.
Columns: TransactionID (int) - (Primary key for TransactionHistory records.), ProductID (int) - (Product identification number. Foreign key to Product.ProductID.), ReferenceOrderID (int) - (Purchase order, sales order, or work order identification number.), ReferenceOrderLineID (int) - (Line number associated with the purchase order, sales order, or work order.), TransactionDate (datetime) - (Date and time of the transaction.), TransactionType (nchar) - (W = WorkOrder, S = SalesOrder, P = PurchaseOrder), Quantity (int) - (Product quantity.), ActualCost (money) - (Product cost.), ModifiedDate (datetime) - (Date and time the record was last updated.)
Relationships:
ProductID -> Production.Product.ProductID
