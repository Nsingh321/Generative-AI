Table: Sales.SalesOrderHeaderSalesReason
Description: Cross-reference table mapping sales orders to sales reason codes.
Columns: SalesOrderID (int) - (Primary key. Foreign key to SalesOrderHeader.SalesOrderID.), SalesReasonID (int) - (Primary key. Foreign key to SalesReason.SalesReasonID.), ModifiedDate (datetime) - (Date and time the record was last updated.)
Relationships:
SalesOrderID -> Sales.SalesOrderHeader.SalesOrderID
SalesReasonID -> Sales.SalesReason.SalesReasonID
